# Your Time Is Worth
### What is the time cost of your purchase?

You input your pay rate and how much you wish to spend, and the app replies how much time your purchase will cost.

Try it out at https://dannyhand.us/time/

## Build for Android
Do: `GitHub > Actions > Build Android APK`

 - Uses Capacitor to produce apk.
 - Pre-built apk is available in Releases.
